Game Rules & Grammar Guide

Key Article Pronunciation Rules:

  • A: Used before words starting with a CONSONANT SOUND (e.g., a European tour [/j/ sound], a university).
  • AN: Used before words starting with a VOWEL SOUND (e.g., an hour [/h/ is silent], an honest person, an MBA degree [/em/ sound]).
  • THE: Used for specific, unique, or previously identified items/people in context.

How to Play Baamboozle:

  1. Divide the class into up to 5 teams. Take turns selecting a tile.
  2. Click the tile to reveal the question or Power-Up event.
  3. Answer within the time limit (if applicable).
  4. Teacher reveals answer and clicks Correct or Incorrect to automatically award points and trigger power-up mechanics.
